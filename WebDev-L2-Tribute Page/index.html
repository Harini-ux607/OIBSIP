About Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

Dr. Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was an Indian aerospace scientist and the 11th President of India. He was born on October 15, 1931, in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. He came from a humble background and developed a strong interest in science and learning from an early age.

Kalam worked with India's space and defence programmes and played an important role in the development of India's launch vehicle and missile programmes. His contribution to India's scientific and technological development earned him the popular title "Missile Man of India."

In 2002, Kalam became the 11th President of India. During his presidency, he was widely admired for his simple personality, interaction with students and encouragement of young people to pursue education, science and innovation.

After completing his presidential term, Kalam continued to teach, write and interact with students across the country. He remained an inspiring figure for young people until his death on July 27, 2015, while delivering a lecture at the Indian Institute of Management Shillong.
